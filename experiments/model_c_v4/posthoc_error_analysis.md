*Note: This analysis relies strictly on the immutable, frozen predictions of the V4 test evaluation. No model parameters, thresholds, or architectural decisions were altered in response to these findings.*

## 2. Impersonation Errors (False Negatives)
**Total Errors**: 27 (22 predicted Genuine, 5 predicted Different Person)

- **Observed**: All 27 missed impersonations were generated by `inswapper_128`. The correctly classified impersonations exhibited a mean `P(Synthetic)` of `0.9360`, whereas the missed impersonations exhibited a severely degraded mean `P(Synthetic)` of `0.1704`. The mean `P(Identity_Match)` for these failures remained high (`0.8414`).
- **Supported Interpretation**: The 2D Fusion classifier failed because the visual branch failed. The EfficientNet backbone did not detect sufficient synthetic artifacts to trigger a high `P(Synthetic)` score, causing the fusion layer to interpret the high identity match as a Genuine presentation.
- **Hypothesis requiring further investigation**: These specific source videos may contain motion blur, heavy compression, or low-lighting conditions that effectively destroy or mask the high-frequency upsampling artifacts typically left by the InSwapper generator.

## 3. Genuine Errors
**Total Errors**: 41 (35 predicted Different Person, 6 predicted Impersonation)

- **Observed**: The mean `P(Identity_Match)` for correctly classified Genuine samples is `0.9602`. For the 41 misclassified Genuine samples, it crashes to `0.3759`.
- **Supported Interpretation**: The V4 model misclassifies these Genuine samples primarily because the frozen ArcFace embeddings fail to recognize the probe face as matching the reference identity. Because `P(Synthetic)` remains correctly low (`0.1419`), the fusion layer logically defaults to `Different Person`.
- **Hypothesis requiring further investigation**: These test samples likely contain extreme yaw/pitch head poses, heavy occlusion, or severe lighting discrepancies between the reference enrollment image and the live probe image, causing the baseline ArcFace cosine similarity to drop below the calibrated identity threshold.

## 4. Different Person Errors
**Total Errors**: 2 (2 predicted Impersonation, 0 predicted Genuine)

- **Observed**: The `P(Identity_Match)` for these two errors remained correctly low (`0.17` and `0.07`, consistent with the class mean of `0.12`). However, their `P(Synthetic)` scores spiked to `0.80` and `0.65` respectively.
- **Supported Interpretation**: The visual branch emitted a strong False Positive for synthetic manipulation. The fusion MLP, encountering a low identity match but a very high synthetic probability, opted to classify them as Impersonations rather than strictly Different Persons.
- **Hypothesis requiring further investigation**: The original unmanipulated source frames may contain bizarre digital artifacts, heavy ISP sharpening, or compression blocking that perfectly mimics the spatial frequencies of deepfake generation.

## 7. Validation vs. Test Distribution Comparison
An observational comparison between Phase 7F.13 Validation and Phase 7F.15 Test reveals profound stability:

- **Genuine P(Id_Match)**: Val `0.82` → Test `0.89`
- **Different Person P(Id_Match)**: Val `0.12` → Test `0.12`
- **Impersonation P(Synth)**: Val `0.66` → Test `0.72`

**Conclusion**: The test distribution is broadly consistent with the validation distribution. In fact, the slight increase in class separation (higher genuine identity matches and higher synthetic probabilities on test) perfectly explains the observed 7% bump in test accuracy. There is absolutely no evidence of adverse distribution shift.

## Limitations
1. **Visual False Negatives**: When the visual branch fails to detect artifacts, V4 defaults to trusting the identity branch (predicting Genuine). 
2. **ArcFace Brittleness**: 85% of Genuine errors are caused directly by ArcFace failing to match unconstrained probe faces to their enrollment references.
3. **Fusion MLP Extrapolation**: The fusion layer occasionally struggles with conflicting edge-cases (e.g., classifying a `Low Id + High Synth` sample as Impersonation instead of Different Person). 

*This concludes Phase 7F.16. No retraining or calibration tuning was performed.*
