## 5. Primary Scientific Finding
**V2 Impersonation Recall vs C-Adv V1**
In the C-Adv V1 experiment, the model achieved near-perfect impersonation recall on the test set. However, that performance was driven entirely by a geometric bounding-box / padding artifact present in the unaligned V1 training data.

In this C-Adv V2 experiment, the geometric leakage was strictly removed by identically aligning the face crops across all three classes. 

**Result**: Without the structural shortcut, the adversarial model completely collapses on the clean validation set. **Impersonation recall fell to exactly 0.00%**. Of the 96 clean impersonation samples in the validation set, 74 were confidently misclassified as `genuine` and 22 as `different_person`. 

This scientifically proves that the EfficientNet-B0 backbone under this training regime is fundamentally incapable of detecting the deep synthetic artifacts generated by Native InSwapper at 112x112 resolution. The adversarial training protocol (FGSM epsilon 0.05) failed to force the model to learn valid anti-impersonation features once the data leakage was closed.
